No site in this list matches. Open any Discourse forum or WordPress site in Forumind, and it tells you whether the site works.

Discourse forums

Any Discourse forum on any domain, public or login-only. Sign in inside the app and it reads what you can read.

WordPress sites

Posts and comments through the site’s public REST API (/wp-json/wp/v2/). Sites that turn it off are not supported.

Hacker News

Stories and comments through Algolia’s public HN API. Read only: no sign-in, posting or voting.

Not sure if another site works? Open it in Forumind and the app tells you. Examples only; Forumind is not affiliated with these sites.

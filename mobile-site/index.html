Ask what the summary left out

Why can’t I post more than two links yet?

New accounts (trust level 0) are limited to two links per post. It’s a spam guard, not a bug.

How long does that usually take?

For most people, a day or two of normal reading and a few genuine replies. There’s no application or waiting queue.

Ask the forum, and it searches the forum itself

It uses the forum’s own search, the way you would: it picks the keywords, opens the topics that match, refines the query and searches again when it needs to, then answers with numbered sources from that forum. Tap S1 to open the post. You can watch every step, and it only reads; it never posts.

Retry-After backoff streaming

Most 429s during streaming come from retrying too fast S1. The fix people report most is exponential backoff from the Retry-After header S2; batching tool calls helps too, since each call counts toward the same limit S3.

Sources

On Discourse and WordPress sites it uses the site’s own search; on Hacker News, Algolia’s HN Search. By default a question gets up to 15 steps and 8 topic reads; you can change both in Settings.

Your model, your key, your data

Keep one model for summaries and chat, and another for Ask the forum.

You stay in charge of your data

  • No Forumind server, no sign-up. No account, analytics, ads or tracking. The developer collects nothing.
  • Straight to the provider you chose. Posts and your questions go from your device to the AI you picked, with your own API key. Keys are kept in the iOS Keychain, and in iCloud Keychain if you sync them.
  • It asks first. Before the first request to each provider, the app shows what is sent and waits for your answer. Say no and nothing is sent; you can withdraw later in Settings.
  • Options that stay close. Apple Intelligence runs on your device or on Apple’s Private Cloud Compute. Ollama and LM Studio talk only to your own computer. Translation and the device’s voices run on the device.
  • Your history is yours. Summaries and chats stay on your device, and sync end-to-end encrypted in your own iCloud. Delete per forum or everything at once.
  • Block and report. Block a user, or report a post, a topic, a user or AI output. Blocked posts and accounts stay out of AI requests.

        It's a rolling window, and a high-traffic day is no longer included.

        Consider this sequence

        M   T  W  T  F  S  S  M
10 [50 11 10 13 10 18 10]

        The next day, that 50 on Tuesday goes out of range, so the sum will decrease even with the same number of days

        M  T   W  T  F  S  S  M  T
10 50 [11 10 13 10 18 10 12]

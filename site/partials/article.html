Harbour towns bet on night ferries to ease the summer rush

By Ana Reyes · 6 min read

The ferry office in Saltby opened its night timetable in May, and by the second weekend the 23:40 sailing ran full.

Northwick followed in June with two crossings after ten. The council’s transport lead called it a test that can be stopped in a week.

Three more towns plan to start next summer. Their harbours need lighting along the quay and a second ticket window.

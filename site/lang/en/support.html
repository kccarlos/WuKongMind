Common questions

Does it cost anything?

WukongMind has no accounts, subscriptions or in-app purchases. The AI provider you connect may charge for usage. Apple Intelligence and models on your own network cost nothing to use.

Do I need an API key?

To use the Agent you connect an AI: your own key with a hosted provider, a model on your local network, or Apple Intelligence on devices that have it. Browsing works without any of these.

Which sites work?

Any web page. The Agent reads and changes whatever page you have open.

The Agent changed a page and I want it back.

Reload the page. If the Agent saved the change as a script, switch it off or delete it in Settings › Scripts.

Where does my data go?

Nowhere but to the AI provider you chose, when you use the Agent. There is nothing to sign up for and nothing is collected. The details are in the privacy policy.

Is the source available?

Yes, on GitHub.

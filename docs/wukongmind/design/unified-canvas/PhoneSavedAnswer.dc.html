Which fanless boxes do people regret?

Mostly the ones with soldered eMMC storage. Two owners say theirs wore out within two years of logging 1 2.

  • Boxes with an M.2 slot are the usual fix: swap the drive, keep the box 3.
  • Throttling in a warm closet is a complaint, not a regret; a timed USB fan solves it 1.

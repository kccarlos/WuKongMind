Forumind helps you catch up on long forum threads. It's a forum browser with an AI assistant beside it: summarize long topics, ask follow-up questions, and let the assistant search the whole forum for you, with links to the posts it used. It works with any Discourse forum, WordPress sites and Hacker News.

SKIP THE SCROLL
Get the main points of a 500-reply topic without reading every post: the original post, what people are saying, and tips from the thread, with post numbers you can tap. Later, tap Check for new replies and only the new posts are read.

ASK QUESTIONS
Chat about the topic you're reading: "What did people decide?" or "Is there a workaround?" Edit a question to try it again, and set instructions just for that topic.

ASK THE FORUM
Ask a question and the assistant searches the forum, reads the most relevant topics, and answers with numbered sources you can tap to check.

DISCOURSE, WORDPRESS AND HACKER NEWS
Open any Discourse forum and the app recognizes it; if a forum needs you to log in, log in right in the app, and your login is sent only to that forum. WordPress sites work through their public REST API: read a post and its reader comments. For Hacker News, the assistant reads stories and comments through the public Algolia API, while the in-app browser shows the site itself. Pin the sites you use most.

TODAY: DIGEST AND PODCAST
The Today page collects new replies in topics you watch and the top topics on your pinned forums. Turn it into a short two-host podcast, read by your device's voices or, with your own Google key, Google's voices. Any summary can also be read aloud.

TALK TO THE PAGE
Live voice lets you ask about the page you're reading out loud, using Google Gemini Live with your own key. It asks for your consent first, because your voice and the page are sent to Google.

TRANSLATE
Translate the posts on screen, or a summary, on your device (iOS 18 or later).

SHARE, SIRI AND SPOTLIGHT
Share any forum page from Safari or Chrome to Forumind and pick what to do with it; open Forumind and it's waiting. Use Siri and Shortcuts to summarize a link, ask a forum, open today's digest, play the podcast or start a voice chat. Topics you've read can appear in Spotlight (optional).

WATCH TOPICS
Get a notification when a topic you care about has new replies.

SAFE BROWSING
Report a post or topic, block a user so their posts disappear everywhere in the app, and hide posts that contain words you choose.

YOUR CHOICE OF AI
Use Apple Intelligence where it's available: no account and no key needed. Or bring your own AI provider with an API key, including many popular cloud services, or run a model on your own computer. Before anything goes to a provider for the first time, the app tells you what is sent and asks.

KIND TO FORUMS
Forumind reads a topic one page per second by default, so it never hammers a forum's servers; you can change the pace in Settings. Ad and tracker blocking (EasyList and EasyPrivacy) is optional and off by default, out of respect for forum owners who rely on ads.

ON ALL YOUR DEVICES
Built for iPhone and iPad, with the forum and the assistant side by side on iPad. Your forums, summaries, chats and settings sync through your own iCloud account, end-to-end encrypted.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
- No accounts, no analytics, no ads, no tracking.
- There are no Forumind servers. The app talks directly to the sites you open and to the AI you choose, and once a day downloads our public moderation list from GitHub.
- API keys are stored in your Keychain.
- Your history stays on your devices and in your own iCloud.

# Content Rights & Availability

App Store Connect › App Information › Content Rights and Pricing & Availability.

## Content Rights

- **Does your app contain, show, or access third-party content?** `Yes`
- **Do you have all the necessary rights to display this third-party content?** `Yes`
- **Basis:** Bundled ad and tracker blocking filter lists derived from EasyList and EasyPrivacy (CC BY-SA 3.0), credited in `NOTICE` and in the app under Settings › Acknowledgements. Web pages are loaded directly from user-chosen URLs as a general web browser.

## Pricing & Availability

- **Price:** Free (`0.00`), no in-app purchases, no subscriptions.
- **Territories:** All territories **except China mainland** (consistent with Forumind, due to local AI service and web filtering regulations).
- **Categories:**
  - Primary: `UTILITIES` (`appstore/metadata/primary_category.txt`)
  - Secondary: `PRODUCTIVITY` (`appstore/metadata/secondary_category.txt`)

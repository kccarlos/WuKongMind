> Fable is good, but you really have to push the ambition of your prompt in order to see proper separation from Opus 5.5.

I love seeing this verbalized. We are pretty much at the point that increased model capability will only have utility to folks that are willing to let go of even more understanding of whatever the model is producing.

The best models can/will produce a year's worth of output that would take several days to weeks to properly understand in the span of an afternoon.

For everyone that still wants to feel like they own the end result, just use the lesser models with less ambitious tasks.

Caveat: I think this mainly applies to coding. I would still like the best models possible to be working on e.g. new drug research to cure our various ailments.
